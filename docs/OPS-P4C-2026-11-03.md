## Endpoints (worker/ops-money.js, routed in worker/ops.js)
| Method | Path | What | Cache |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/ops/finance?range=30d\|90d\|12m` | revenue by day/week/month split package/addon/hostess/design, P&L + gross/net margin per month, vendor costs, fixed costs, ad spend, manual vendor entries, per-event economics, invoices, cash view, MoM growth, cumulative | KV `opsfin:<range>` 5 min |
| POST | `/api/ops/finance` `{set_manual:{month,vendor,ils}}` | manual vendor cost → `costman:<YYYY-MM>` (cloudflare, monday, make, anthropic, telnyx, retell, other) | busts cache |
| POST | `/api/ops/invoice-resend` `{token[,confirm]}` | re-sends an EXISTING invoice link (template `invoicetmpl`). No confirm = preview. confirm = token's first 8 chars. Refuses 21:00–09:00 | — |
| GET | `/api/ops/ads?range=` | campaign → ad set → ad: spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, leads, CPL, ROAS; blended ROAS; traffic; Shalev campaign `read_only:true` | `opsads:<range>` 10 min |
| GET | `/api/ops/templates` | all templates on the 3 WABAs (guests 1378764257421712, portfolio 2815615772154486, old 1060242146337688) + sends/delivered/read/failed (template-stats, 30 days) + pending age | `opstpl` 10 min |
| POST | `/api/ops/template-variant` `{waba,name,new_name[,confirm]}` | copies a template's components under a new name and submits to Meta. confirm = new_name | busts `opstpl` |
| GET | `/api/ops/feedback?group=week\|month` | fb: list, per-event roll-up, review-page link, weekly average trend, NPS | `opsfb:<group>` 5 min |

All GETs take `?fresh=1`. Fixed costs and ad spend are written through the existing `/api/fixedcost` and `/api/adspend`.

## Exact vs estimate
Exact: Grow pay-record sums, sheet sums for paid events, ad spend from Meta insights, the adspend/fixedcost/costman KV values, CRM hostess pay, template statuses, feedback scores, Meta WA spend (pricing_analytics, USD).
Estimates (marked ≈ in the UI): (1) a package payment that includes hostesses or design is split at list price (HOSTESS_PRICE / DESIGN_PRICE). (2) WhatsApp ₪ on days with no `wacost:` uses templates × $0.0053. (3) Calls ₪ = Retell USD × 3.7. No separate Telnyx per-call cost was found to export, so Telnyx is included only where Retell's combined cost has it. (4) Per-event message cost = evcost − audited call cost. (5) Attributed ROAS only counts CRM leads whose `origin.campaign` matches the campaign name. Blended ROAS = all revenue ÷ all spend. (6) Revenue before 22/10 (when pay audit rows begin) comes from the sheet and counts as package revenue.

# Store rules read on 20/11/2026 (primary pages, fetched that day)

Policy text is not reviewer behaviour. Items marked (reading) are my interpretation.

## Apple · https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- 4.2: "Your app should include features, content, and UI that elevate it beyond a repackaged website."
- 4.5.4: "Push Notifications must not be required for the app to function"; no promotions without explicit opt-in in the app's UI, with an opt-out.
- 5.1.1(v): "If your app supports account creation, you must also offer account deletion within the app."
  Support FAQ: linking out to the browser "to sign in or register an account provides a poor user experience and isn't appropriate, per App Review Guideline 4." https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/
  (reading) a sign-in-only app is outside the literal rule; we offer deletion in the app anyway.
- 2.1(a): "include demo account info (and turn on your back-end service!) if your app includes a login." Demo account "must not expire". https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-review-information/
- 3.1.1: unlocking features in the app needs in-app purchase. 3.1.3(e): "physical goods or services that will be consumed outside of the app, you must use purchase methods other than in-app purchase". Apps may not, in the app, encourage other purchase methods (outside the US storefront). (reading) version 1 shows no purchase UI at all.
- Review time: "at least 50% of submissions in less than 24 hours and 90% in less than 48 hours." https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review/
- Enrolment (individual): "Your name will be displayed as the seller name of your apps on the App Store." https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/ Verification duration: not published. Organisations need a D-U-N-S number.
- DSA: trader status must be declared by everyone; distributing "only outside the EU" means "you're not acting as a trader". Individual traders show address, phone and email on EU product pages. https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/
- Uploads: "must be built with Xcode 26 or later using an SDK for iOS 26". https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/
- Screenshots: at least one iPhone size is required; iPad only "if app runs on iPad". Exact sizes: recheck in App Store Connect (least certain item). https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications/
- Privacy manifest required for listed SDKs incl. Capacitor. https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/
- Export: HTTPS-only apps set `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` NO. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/complying-with-encryption-export-regulations
- Privacy labels: Name, Phone Number, User ID linked to the user; guest lists → Contacts (conservative); push token → Device ID; no tracking. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Age rating: new 4+/9+/13+/16+/18+ questionnaire is mandatory. https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/

## Google
- "Developers with personal accounts created after November 13, 2023, must run a closed test for their app with a minimum of 12 testers who have been opted in continuously for at least 14 days." Production-access review "usually takes seven days or less, but can occasionally take longer." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465
- Account deletion: "If your app enables account creation, you must: provide users with an in-app path to delete their app accounts… and provide a web link resource"; also when the app directs users to an outside creation flow. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111
- "Starting August 31, 2026: New apps and app updates must target Android 16 (API level 36) or higher". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878
- Personal accounts: "Google displays your legal name, your country… and your developer email address on Google Play. If you monetize on Google Play, Google displays your full address." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312
- Data safety: collect = "transmitting data from your app off a user's device". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- WebView / minimum functionality: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9899034 , /answer/9898783
- Not fetched: the Android developer verification page.

## Tech
- Capacitor 8: Node 22+, Xcode 26+, Android Studio Otter 2025.2.1+, iOS 15 minimum, SPM by default. https://capacitorjs.com/docs/updating/8-0
- Push plugin returns the APNs token on iOS and the FCM token on Android. https://capacitorjs.com/docs/apis/push-notifications
- APNs: HTTP/2 + TLS 1.2, `api.push.apple.com` / `api.sandbox.push.apple.com`, `/3/device/<token>`, headers `authorization: bearer <jwt>`, `apns-topic`, `apns-push-type`; JWT ES256 with `kid`, `iss`, `iat`, reused 20–60 minutes. https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/sending-notification-requests-to-apns
- FCM HTTP v1 with a service-account OAuth token (RS256). https://firebase.google.com/docs/cloud-messaging/send/v1-api
- NOT verified: a Cloudflare Worker reaching APNs over HTTP/2.
